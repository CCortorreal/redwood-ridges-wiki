# Founding Day Mega Pack

> Goes up at the **2pm ceremony on Founding Day** (Saturday, 26 September). Everything below is
> what will be on the board, not what's there yet — check back after the ceremony, or watch
> `#contracts` for the threads as they open.

The board wall fills all at once, right at the ceremony: **23 jobs**, six of them mega builds.
Every job was somebody's idea first — the "idea by" name is who asked for it.

**Crew jobs need their whole crew before the payout lands.** A few of the biggest jobs list
build stages on their card (like "Walls and roof," then "Stocked") — those are just progress
markers. The purse pays once, when the whole job is finished and checked, not per stage.

## What these places will do once they're finished

Every job promises what its place will *do*, not just how it'll look:

- **The Stockyard** becomes the camp's delivery point — sort your goods in, and future
  procurement jobs deliver here.
- **New Spawn** becomes where every new player arrives, with paths back to camp, the board, and
  the market.
- **The Temple of Mallow** takes offerings once it's finished and switched on — see
  [the Field Guide's landmark note](guide/01-where-youve-landed.md) below.
- **The Post Office** gives everyone a mailbox — see the same note for what that means.
- **Any finished job here can carry a builders' plaque**, crediting whoever built it and whoever
  asked for it.
