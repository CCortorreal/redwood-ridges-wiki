# Chapter 2 — Marshmallows, the Camp's Money

**Marshmallows** pay for towns, claims, and other camp business. You can trade items or favors however you want.

### One Civic Currency

Only the camp government can create or destroy Marshmallows.
You can't craft or farm any Marshmallows yourself.
Spend them on towns, land claims, and opening new territory.
Trade items, work, or favors however your group wants.
The server has no `/sell` command that turns dirt into money.
Players trade with each other at the market and the auction house (Chapter 13).

### Your First Hundred

Every verified player receives one grant of **100** Marshmallows when joining.
That starts you off, but won't fund a town alone.
The `/pay` command moves existing money between players without creating more.
Alt accounts don't qualify for another starter grant.

- **Check Balance:** `/bal`
- **Pay a Player:** `/pay`

### How Money Enters

Money enters the server through these four approved sources:
- a one-time starter grant;
- verified public-works contracts;
- fixed prizes from published events;
- voting grants after public launch.
Closed alpha doesn't include any voting grants yet.
Unclaimed contract money never gets added to the economy.
