# Chapter 4 — Founding a Town

A town protects shared land and holds a shared bank. You'll need a group to found one together.

### Scout Before Mayor

Saving money alone won't let you found a town.
Join a town, or finish one paid job from the board.
Either one makes you a **Scout**.
Grinding, playtime, and skill levels don't count toward Scout.
Money sent through `/pay` doesn't count toward Scout either.
Only players with Scout rank can use `/t new`.
Founding a town makes you a **Ranger** at once (Chapter 8).

- **Pay the Founder:** `/pay`
- **Found a Town:** `/t new`

### Pool the Money

Creating a new town costs **400 Marshmallows** in total.
Each newcomer starts with 100, so pool starter grants and earned pay with friends.
Choose one Scout to hold the money and create the town.
Stand exactly where you want the town center before using the command.

- **Invite a Player:** `/t add`
- **Accept Invite:** `/accept`

### Joining a Town

Every town has a charter that says how newcomers join.
Open **Find a Town**, read a town's charter, and petition to join.
The mayor can say yes even while you're offline; a letter brings the answer.
Some towns let you in at once if you hold a civic rank (Passport), and some let 2 residents vouch for you (Vouch).
Seedlings can join towns, and joining one makes you a **Scout**.
A mayor can still invite you directly with `/t add`; accept with `/accept [Town]`.

- **Find a Town:** `/charter towns`
- **Accept Invite:** `/accept`
- **Town Info:** `/t`

### The Hall of Towns

The Hall of Towns, just west of spawn at (-510, 70, -2350), holds every town's charter in one place.
Right-click its board to read the charters and petition from there.
A petition waits up to **3 days**; the mayor gets a reminder letter after 2.
If it lapses, your letter names towns that are still recruiting.

- **Find a Town:** `/charter towns`

### Run Your Charter

Mayors and assistants open the charter desk with `/charter`.
Write a pitch and choose how people join: Manual, Passport or Vouch.
Manual means you or an assistant approve each petition, whenever you're on.
Stock a chest with one welcome kit per row, then set it from the desk.
Each newcomer gets the next kit by post; the town bank pays the stamp: 1, plus about 0.7 per filled slot (a full row of 9 is 8, never more than 20).

- **Charter Desk:** `/charter`
- **Fund Town Bank:** `/t deposit`
- **Town Spawn:** `/t spawn`

### Fund and Return

Residents add money to the town bank with `/t deposit`.
The bank pays for claims and daily upkeep.
Use `/t spawn` to return home; it has a 10-minute cooldown.
Use `/t` to open the town's info card.
