# Chapter 20 — Help & Staff

Stuck, confused, or something's wrong? Here's who to ask and how.

### Ask the Guide

Open **My Camp** from the Field Guide for your own page: rank, wallet, town, mail and open jobs.
**Ask the Guide** searches every page of this book.
Type a word like *stamp*, *grave* or *charter* and jump straight to the page.

- **My Camp:** `/guide`
- **Ask the Guide:** `/guide find`

### Getting Help

Press **Ask for Help** and type what's wrong.
Every staff member who's online sees it right away.
If nobody's on, send a report instead; the Wardens read reports on Discord.

- **Ask for Help:** `/guide help`
- **Report a Problem:** `/rreport`

### Reporting a Problem

**Report a Problem** opens a private form that goes to the Wardens.
Your name is optional, and the Wardens never see which account sent it.
Say what happened and where. You can send one report every few minutes.

### Who the Staff Are

Staff are players who help run the camp, and their rank is separate from your civic rank.
**Helpers** answer questions and pass reports up to the Wardens.
**Wardens** can mute, kick, and roll back grief after they've looked at the evidence.
Carlos is the **Head of Moderation** and makes the final call on bans and appeals.
Staff never hand out items, change claims, or touch anyone's money.

### Joining Staff

Staff join through applications, which open when the camp needs more help.
Watch the Discord announcements; when a window opens, use `/staff-apply` there.
You'll want a couple of weeks on the server and the **Scout** rank first.
