# Chapter 10 — Talking, Voice & Chat

Use nearby voice to hang out, and use text when the camp needs a record.

### Proximity Voice

Simple Voice Chat runs on the Redwood Ridges server.
The camp's modpack includes the client mod you need.
Nearby players sound close, then fade as you move apart.
You never need voice chat to claim a contract.
Put claims and payout splits in text so everyone can check them.

### Camp Discord

The camp Discord holds official notices and offline plans.
`#campfire` is the camp's general community hangout channel. Chat there reaches the game and the other way round, and its header shows who's online.
`#contracts` has one thread per board job and follows the board as it changes.
`#the-world` keeps a card for every town and nation: mayor, residents, open or closed.
`#auction-house` shows what's listed at the Auctioneer and what sells.
`#server-status` shows server uptime and the current player count.
Union Rep provides help, map, and group commands.
Minecraft ranks don't sync to Discord roles yet.

- [Open Camp Discord](https://discord.gg/TqPYyJcdXF)

### When Recording Is Active

The camp announces every filming window ahead of time.
You can play without appearing in any video.
You can always stay off voice and camera.
Tell Carlos privately if you don't want to be featured.
A video needs your approval before focusing on your voice, skin, or build.
