# Chapter 6 — The Treeline

The **treeline** is the edge of known territory, which the whole camp moves together.

### The Edge of the Known World

The current world is **3,000 blocks wide**, giving it a **1,500-block radius** centered on spawn.
The red wall marks the current treeline by design.
The server has already prepared the land beyond it.
Only the camp's treeline ceremony opens that prepared land.
The Nether border tracks the overworld at a 1:8 ratio — **375 blocks wide**.

- [See the Current World](http://104.204.220.59:8100)

### When the Forest Is Ready

Before a push, at least one stable town must pay upkeep on time.
Players should have explored most of the open ground.
They also need to be asking for more land.
The ledger must show a real surplus of saved Marshmallows.
The Head Ranger checks all four conditions before calling the event.
A date alone can't trigger a treeline event.

### The Call to Campfire

The announcement arrives three to five days before the push.
It gives the date and destination of the new ring.
It lists one total price for everyone together.
The public ledger shows exactly where that price came from.
The board and Discord carry the same announcement.
Each ring gets its own price from the ledger.

- [Open Camp Discord](https://discord.gg/TqPYyJcdXF)
- [Open BlueMap](http://104.204.220.59:8100)

### Who Does What

The Head Ranger posts the event and tracks the total.
Contributors give any amount of Marshmallows they can spare.
Scouts bring the camp's towns and crews together.
Explorers watch the new land for its first ruins.
Builders mark safe routes leading back to camp.
Everyone gets full access, even if they couldn't contribute.

### The Funding Round

Meet at the campfire when the funding round begins.
Say your contribution where everyone else can hear it.
The Head Ranger removes that amount from your balance.
The push begins only after contributions meet the full price.
Don't send your contribution through another player as a proxy.
Keep enough money in your town bank for upkeep.

- **Check Balance:** `/bal`
- **Check Town Bank:** `/t`

### The Push and the Rush

The Head Ranger announces, *the treeline pushes*, when funding is complete.
Wait until the red border wall starts moving outward.
Then crews can cross into the new land together.
Share the first ruins, dungeons, and landmarks your crew discovers.
Don't glitch through the border before the wall moves.
Don't build against the wall while it's moving.
The first steps into new land are the ceremony's payoff.
