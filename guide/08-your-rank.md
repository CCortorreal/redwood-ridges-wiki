# Chapter 8 — Your Rank

Your rank is your standing in the camp. It climbs as you do paid work for the Ridge.

### The Civic Ladder

The civic ladder runs **Seedling → Scout → Ranger → Pathfinder → Scout Master**.
Every new player on the server starts as a Seedling.
Your first paid board job, or joining a town, makes you a **Scout**.
Ranks are about standing, not power: none of them makes you staff.
**Wardens** are the camp's moderators, a separate trust role the operator hands out by name.
A shield ⛨ beside a name marks camp staff: silver for a Warden, gold for the operator. They are on watch for the camp, so ask them for help.

### Where You Stand

Type `/rank` any time to see how close you are to your next rank.
It shows a checklist: what's done, what's left, and what the next rank unlocks.
After every paid board job, a bar at the top of your screen fills toward your next rank.
Each job card on the board shows what that job adds to your climb.
If the days on the Ridge are all that's left, `/rank` tells you the date your rank lands.
**My Camp** in `/guide` shows the same checklist.

- **My Rank:** `/rank`

### Climbing It

The board keeps a lifetime count of the Marshmallows you've been paid for board work.

| Rank | Earned from contracts | Time on the Ridge | Also needs |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Scout** | your first paid job, or joining a town | — | — |
| **Ranger** | 400 | 14 days | — or skip both: found a town |
| **Pathfinder** | 1,200 | 45 days | 40 advancements, and roots: 40 different contracts finished, 30 days as a town resident, or mayor of a town with 2 other residents |
| **Scout Master** | 3,000 | 120 days | 80 advancements and a clean record, confirmed by staff |

Scout, Ranger and Pathfinder land on their own the moment you cross the bar. Scout Master never does.
Most of the camp should settle at Scout and Ranger; Pathfinder is for the regulars who've put roots down.

### What Each Rank Unlocks

Each rank keeps everything from the ranks below it.

| Rank | Board jobs at once | Second-rune chance | Also unlocks |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Seedling** | 1 | 25% | chat, voice, `/pay`, shops, and joining a town |
| **Scout** | 2 | 35% | founding a town (`/t new`), a colored nickname (`/nick`), particle effects (`/pp`), two more tags (`/tags`) |
| **Ranger** | 3 | 45% | 3 homes, every tag, a gradient nickname |
| **Pathfinder** | 5 | 55% | — |
| **Scout Master** | 8 | 60% | — |

Your Discord rank role follows your in-game rank, if your Discord account is linked through `/apply`.

### What Does Not Promote You

Playtime and your Marshmallow balance won't change your rank.
Money sent through `/pay` won't move you up, and neither will skills.
Town jobs, dungeon pay and Treeline offerings pay out, but they don't count toward rank.
Only paid board work counts, and the camp confirms the top rank by hand.
