# Chapter 19 — Post & Mail

Letters are free and instant. Parcels carry a small stamp and land straight in your mailbox.

### Your Mailbox

Mailboxes are **barrels**. Put a trapdoor on top: that's the flag.
Stand on the barrel at your own place and type `/mailbox`.
That barrel is now your mailbox, and only you can open it.
It has to sit on a plot you own or on your own town's land.
When mail lands, the flag pops up. Open the box and it drops again.
Run `/mailbox` on another barrel to move it; `/mailbox clear` removes it.
With no barrel under you, `/mailbox` tells you where yours is.
A chest that was already your mailbox keeps working.

- **Where's My Mailbox:** `/mailbox`

### Shared Mailboxes

Live together? Share one box.
The owner types `/mailbox share <name>`; the other player then types `/mailbox join <owner>` within 5 minutes.
Mail for either of you lands in the same barrel, and both of you can open it.
`/mailbox unshare <name>` takes someone off.

### Homestead Mailboxes

Living out in the wilderness? You can still get mail.
A wilderness barrel can become your mailbox if it's **empty** when you claim it.
That rule stops anyone from claiming a barrel full of someone else's things.

### Auction Mail

Got a mailbox? Your Auction House sales come to you.
When something you listed sells, the marshmallows go straight to your balance and a **Sale receipt** lands in your mailbox.
When a listing ends unsold, the item comes back to your mailbox.
Either way the auction slot frees up at once. If your mailbox is full, the item waits in the Auction House under My Auctions.

### Sending a Parcel

Go to the post office and right-click the **Postmaster**.
The post office shows under **Nearby Places** in `/guide` once staff have opened it.
Pick who it's for, drag items onto the counter, and press **Send**.
Delivery is instant: the parcel lands in their mailbox.
They get a letter saying a parcel arrived.
Closing the counter before you send hands your items back.

### The Stamp

The stamp is 1 Marshmallow plus a little per slot, and never more than 20.

| Parcel size | Marshmallows | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 slot | 2 | a single stack |
| 9 slots | 8 | one row |
| 18 slots | 14 | two rows |
| 27 slots | 20 | a full counter, the cap |

### Dead Letters

If their mailbox is full, broken or gone, the parcel waits in the **dead-letter chest** at the post office.
The stamp is still charged, because the post office did the work.
Open that chest to take back parcels you sent or collect parcels sent to you.
You only ever see your own parcels there.
**My Camp** tells you when something is waiting.

### Letters

Letters are free text messages that wait for you if you're offline.
Towns and the post office write to you by letter: charter answers and parcel notices.

- **Read Letters:** `/mail read`
