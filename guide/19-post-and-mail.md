# Chapter 19 — Post & Mail

Letters are free and instant. Parcels carry a small stamp and land straight in a chest.

### Your Mailbox

Stand on a chest at your own place and type `/mailbox`.
That chest is now your mailbox, and only you can open it.
It has to sit on a plot you own or on your own town's land.
Run `/mailbox` on another chest to move it; `/mailbox clear` removes it.
With no chest under you, `/mailbox` tells you where yours is.

- **Where's My Mailbox:** `/mailbox`

### Homestead Mailboxes

Living out in the wilderness? You can still get mail.
A wilderness chest can become your mailbox if it's **empty** when you claim it.
That rule stops anyone from claiming a chest full of someone else's things.

### Sending a Parcel

Go to the post office and right-click the **Postmaster**.
The post office shows under **Nearby Places** in `/guide` once staff have opened it.
Pick who it's for, drag items onto the counter, and press **Send**.
Delivery is instant: the parcel lands in their mailbox chest.
They get a letter saying a parcel arrived.
Closing the counter before you send hands your items back.

### The Stamp

The stamp is 1 Marshmallow plus a little per slot, and never more than 20.

| Parcel size | Marshmallows | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 slot | 2 | a single stack |
| 9 slots | 8 | one row |
| 18 slots | 14 | two rows |
| 27 slots | 20 | a full counter, the cap |

### Dead Letters

If their mailbox is full, broken or gone, the parcel waits in the **dead-letter chest** at the post office.
The stamp is still charged, because the post office did the work.
Open that chest to take back parcels you sent or collect parcels sent to you.
You only ever see your own parcels there.
**My Camp** tells you when something is waiting.

### Letters

Letters are free text messages that wait for you if you're offline.
Towns and the post office write to you by letter: charter answers and parcel notices.

- **Read Letters:** `/mail read`
