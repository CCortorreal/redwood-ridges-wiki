# Chapter 11 — The Map

Use BlueMap for the shape of the world and your minimap for the next bend in the trail.

### The Big Picture

You can open BlueMap inside any regular web browser.
BlueMap shows the terrain across every rendered dimension.
A Towny addon overlays the borders for towns and claims.
Use it to plan long routes and meetups.
You still need to check the trail in game.

- [Open BlueMap](http://104.204.220.59:8100)

### The Local Picture

Xaero's minimap is included in your client pack.
Save waypoints for homes and worksites you want to find again.
Use `/towny map` to see claim cells around you.
The treeline marks the current limit for exploration.
Share discoveries so the camp's map keeps improving.

- **Nearby Claims:** `/towny map`
