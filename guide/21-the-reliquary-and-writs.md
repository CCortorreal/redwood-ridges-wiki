# Chapter 21 — The Reliquary & Writs

Some grails can't be bought. You earn them, one Writ at a time.

### What a Reliquary Is

A **Reliquary** is a pedestal with a grail turning slowly above it.
The parts that grail needs float in a ring around it.
Each grail has its own Reliquary, and staff set them up.
The first two, the **Paranoia Helm** and **Uno Reverse**, go in when the Grailforge is built.
Walk up close and a count floats over each part.
Parts you've handed in light up. The rest stay dark.

### Read the Writ

Right-click the grail on the pedestal to open its **Writ**.
The Writ tells the grail's story, lists every part it needs, and names everyone who has forged one so far.
On Bedrock, or if clicking is tricky, stand nearby and type `/writ`.
Away from any Reliquary, `/writ` shows the Writs you've taken up.

- **My Writs:** `/writ`

### Take It Up

Press **Read the terms** to see the deal before you agree to anything:
- What you hand in is kept. It never comes back.
- Only 5 of each grail will ever exist, and the Writ closes when all 5 are forged. The first one forged gets #1.
- Each player can forge one of each grail.
- Your name goes into the grail's history, and the whole camp hears about it.

Stand right next to the Reliquary and press **Sign the Writ**.
You get a **Writ book**, and a tracker appears on the side of your screen.

### Hand In Parts

You don't need every part at once. Bring what you have, whenever you have it.
Press **Hand in what I carry**. It shows exactly what it will take first; press **Hand them in** to agree.
It only takes what the Writ still needs, from your pack and your offhand.
Armor you're wearing is never taken.
Each part flies to its spot on the ring and lights up.

### Prove It

When every part is in, you get the **Unfinished** piece. It only works for you.
It starts out weak and gets stronger as you prove it.
The Writ tells you what proving takes, and your tracker keeps count. For the Paranoia Helm it's 200 hostile mobs; for Uno Reverse, 100 shots blocked.
Lost it? Go back to the Reliquary and press **Recast**.
You get a new one, and the old copy stops working.

### Forge It

When the count is full, carry the Unfinished piece back to the Reliquary and press **Forge it**.
You'll see what it takes before anything happens. If forging ever costs Marshmallows, that screen says so.
The grail gets its number, your name joins its history, and the camp hears the news.
If every copy gets forged before you make it back, your Unfinished piece is still yours.

### Your Tracker and Book

Your tracker and your Writ book update as you go, even after you log out.
Carrying two Writs? Press **Track on my sidebar** on the one you want to follow.
`/writ hide` puts the tracker away, and `/writ show` brings it back.
Lost your book? Press **A fresh Writ book** on the Writ for a new one.

- **Hide Tracker:** `/writ hide`
- **Show Tracker:** `/writ show`
