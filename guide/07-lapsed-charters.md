# Chapter 7 — Lapsed Charters

A town that can't pay its upkeep goes into debt first and only then becomes a ruin. Each stage gives you time to recover.

### Before the Charter Lapses

Watch the town bank along with your own balance.
The server takes upkeep from the town bank every day.
Deposit money before the town bank reaches zero.
Before a long break, leave enough in the bank to cover the days you'll be gone.
Catching a bank shortfall early makes recovery cheaper.

- **Town Info:** `/t`
- **Fund Town Bank:** `/t deposit`

### Debt Comes First

When the bank can't pay the upkeep, the town goes **bankrupt** instead of falling at once.
The bank goes into debt, and the town can't claim land, add residents or build until the debt is paid.
Any resident can pay it off with `/t deposit`. The town is back to normal the moment the debt is gone.
A town still bankrupt after **7 days** becomes a ruin.

### Seven Days of Grace

A ruined town gets a **seven-day grace period** before anything is lost permanently.
That covers a weekend when everyone is away.
Use the week to regroup and recover the town.
After it ends, the town is gone and other players may claim the land.
Once a town has been a ruin for **4 hours**, any of its own residents can run `/t reclaim` and pay **200 Marshmallows** to bring it back.
Only the town's residents can reclaim it; outsiders can't take it over while it's a ruin.
If the price here and the server ever disagree, the server wins; ask the Head Ranger.

- **Reclaim a Ruin:** `/t reclaim`
