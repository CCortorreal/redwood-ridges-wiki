# Chapter 23 — Questions Campers Ask

The questions campers ask most, with short answers and where to read more.

### Money

**How much do I have?** Type `/bal`. `/baltop` shows the richest campers.
**How do I earn Marshmallows?** Paid board jobs, town jobs and haul jobs (Chapter 3), first clears of listed dungeons (Chapter 15), and selling at a stall or the Auctioneer (Chapter 13).
**What do I spend them on?** A town and its land, the Treeline, Death's ride back to your grave, and other campers' goods.
**Do Elite Coins turn into Marshmallows?** No, never. They're for the Adventurer's Guild.

- **Check Balance:** `/bal`
- **Open Contracts:** `/contracts`

### Homes and Travel

**How do I set a home?** Stand there and type `/sethome`. `/home` takes you back, and `/homes` lists your homes.
**Is there /tpa?** No, on purpose. Use roads, `/t spawn`, `/home` or `/spawn`.
**Why can't I teleport again?** `/home`, `/spawn` and `/t spawn` each have a 10-minute cooldown.

- **Set Home Here:** `/sethome`
- **Go Home:** `/home`

### Dying

**Where did I die?** Follow the compass you respawn with. Chat also gave you the coordinates.
**How long does my grave last?** 7 days. Only you can open it.
**Can I get there faster?** Pray at Death's statue in the graveyard. The first ride is 25 Marshmallows (Chapter 9).

### Land and Towns

**Can I build without a town?** Yes, anywhere that isn't claimed. Only town claims protect a build, though.
**How do I join a town?** `/charter towns` lists the towns and how each one lets people in (Chapter 4).
**How do I invite a friend to my town?** `/t add <name>`. They accept with `/accept`.
**Do I get money back if I unclaim land?** No.

- **Find a Town:** `/charter towns`
- **Invite a Player:** `/t add`

### Ranks and Jobs

**Why am I still a Seedling?** Finish one paid board job or join a town, and you're a Scout.
**What does Ranger take?** 400 Marshmallows earned from board jobs and 14 days on the Ridge, or found a town.
**How do I check my progress?** `/rank`.
**Where are my jobs?** `/contracts` shows the ones you hold.

- **My Rank:** `/rank`
- **Open Contracts:** `/contracts`

### Playing Together

**Can other players hurt me?** No. PvP is off across the whole server.
**Can I use voice chat?** Yes, on Java. Voices carry 48 blocks (Chapter 10).
**How do I message one person?** `/msg <name> <message>`, and `/r` to answer.
**Can I play on Bedrock?** Yes, but Bedrock players don't get voice chat, Xaero's minimap or the F-key class abilities.
**Something's broken or someone's griefing.** Use **Report a Problem**, or ask in `#campfire`.

- **Ask for Help:** `/guide help`
- **Report a Problem:** `/rreport`
