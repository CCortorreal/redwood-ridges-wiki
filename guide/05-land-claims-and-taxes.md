# Chapter 5 — Land, Claims & Taxes

A **claim** protects one chunk as town land, while the town bank funds that protection.

### Claim the Ground

Stand inside the exact chunk you want to claim.
Running `/t claim` charges **20 Marshmallows** to the town bank.
Claimed land gets Towny protection and can hold plots.
Keep new claims connected instead of scattering them.
Check the claim map before spending any town money.

- **Claim This Chunk:** `/t claim`
- **Nearby Claims:** `/towny map`

### Beyond the Resident Cap

Each resident gives the town **8 claims**.
After that limit, the town can **buy** extra claims with Marshmallows.
The first bonus claim costs **30 Marshmallows**; each later one costs 1.2 times the previous.
The mayor runs `/town buy bonus <number>` and the town bank pays.
How many you may buy grows with the town: up to 16 with 1 resident, 32 with 2, 64 with 6, 96 with 10, 128 with 14, 160 with 20, 192 with 24, and 256 with 28.
An **outpost** is a detached claim that costs **250**.
Buy extra reach because you need it, not on impulse.

- **Buy Bonus Claims:** `/town buy bonus`
- **Town Info:** `/t`

### Feed the Bank

**Upkeep** is your town's daily operating cost: **5 Marshmallows a day**.
The server takes it from the town bank.
Deposit money before the town bank starts running low.
`/t` shows the town bank and its daily upkeep, so you can count the days left.
The server must announce any upkeep rate change first.
If the bank runs dry, Chapter 7 says what happens.

- **Fund Town Bank:** `/t deposit`
- **Town Info:** `/t`

### Taxes

Taxes are off unless the mayor sets one.
A mayor can set a daily tax of up to 1,000 Marshmallows, or up to 25% of each resident's balance. Mayors don't pay their own town's tax.
A resident who can't pay the tax is removed from the town.
Unclaiming land doesn't give back what it cost.
