# Chapter 1 — Where You've Landed

Welcome to **Redwood Ridges**, a small civilization growing out of a ranger camp in the pines.

### The Campground

You start at the campground as a **Seedling**, the rank given to every new player.
Everyone here passed the server's whitelist before joining.
The campfire is where people hang out and make plans.
The ranger station holds the board, where you pick paid jobs.
Players built every cabin, path, and dock you see.
The camp grows as people add to it.

- [Open Camp Discord](https://discord.gg/TqPYyJcdXF)
- [Open BlueMap](http://104.204.220.59:8100)

### A Forever World

Redwood Ridges never resets. There are no map wipes, so what you build stays.
The world grows instead: the border opens outward in rings as the camp funds the Temple of Mallow.

### A Real Account

You need a real Minecraft account: Java or Bedrock.
Cracked clients can't connect; the server checks every login.
If Java is out of reach, Bedrock works too, on a phone, a PC or a console.

### Your First Trail

Start at the board instead of grinding alone.
Click a contract at the board, read it, then press **Claim this job**.
Ask in chat whether another crew needs your help.
Text chat always works, while voice stays optional.
The camp gets better when you build with other people.

### The Camp Tour

New players start the Camp Tour on their first join.
A compass bar at the top of your screen points to the next stop.
Walk into each stop to tick it off; the stops pay **18 Marshmallows** in all.
`/tour` opens your tour log, and `/tour track` hides or shows the compass.
The tour doesn't make you a Scout; a paid board job does.

- **Tour Log:** `/tour`
- **Show or Hide Compass:** `/tour track`

### The Light-Touch Rule

Ranger-camp language adds flavor; you don't need to memorize it.
Costs and commands always stay in plain English.
If a name feels too formal, shorten it in chat.
**The board** means the paid job list at the ranger station.
**The treeline** means the current edge of the open map.
