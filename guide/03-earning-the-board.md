# Chapter 3 — Earning, the Board

The board lists paid work the camp needs.

### Read the Card

Each contract lists the job, payout, deadline, and completion rules.
Some jobs are solo; others need a crew.
Look at a book on the board and its name tells you where it stands: open, who holds it, how many more a crew still needs, or ready for review.
A crew job like "2-4 builders" needs at least its smallest crew on the claim before **Mark ready** works.
Groups choose one payee or agree on the split before starting.
Ask in chat before building if anything seems unclear.

### Claim the Work

Click its book or frame and press **Claim this job**.
`/contracts` shows every open job and the ones you hold.
Your rank sets how many unpaid jobs you can hold at once: Seedling 1, Scout 2, Ranger 3, Pathfinder 5, Scout Master 8.
Changed your mind? Open the book again and press **Give up** so someone else can take it.
Voice helps crews coordinate, but you never need it.
All work happens in survival mode without creative shortcuts.
Check with the Head Ranger before an expensive final step.

### Verification and Payday

When you finish, reopen the card and press **Mark ready**.
The Head Ranger checks the work, then presses **Complete & pay**.
Your first official payout makes you a **Scout**, the next rank after Seedling.
Claiming you're done or sitting AFK won't pay.
Money sent through `/pay` won't change your rank; only official payouts do.

- **Open the Board:** `/contracts`
- **Completed Work:** `/contracts archive`
- **Check Balance:** `/bal`

### Why Prices Move

Contracts follow what the world needs that week.
Spruce wood may pay this week and nothing next week.
Every order has a quantity limit and deadline.
The Head Ranger sets prices by watching what the camp needs.
At this size, that works better than an automatic shop.
