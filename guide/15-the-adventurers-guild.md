# Chapter 15 — The Adventurer's Guild

`/ag` takes you to the **Adventurer's Guild**, a hub world of quests, arenas, and dungeons run by elite mobs.

### Elite Coins

The guild pays in **Elite Coins**, not Marshmallows.
Elite Coins buy guild ranks, guild gear, and arena entries. They never buy towns or land.
Talk to Casus at the guild for the welcome chain; the second quest pays 100 coins.
Finish a quest, then turn it in. Nothing pays until you do.

- **Go to the Guild:** `/ag`
- **Guild Menu:** `/em`

### Elite Mobs and Dungeons

Elite mobs roam the wild with a level over their head. Higher than yours means run.
Guild gear wears out like any other gear. Keep a repair plan.
Story dungeons open from Manager Wallitz; expeditions from Captain Squalus.
Dungeon loot is guild loot. Real diamonds still come from the ground.

### Play Fair

Dungeon runs are survival like everything else here.
Sharing a dungeon with your crew is the point; the guild scales with you.
Report a stuck portal or a broken quest in chat. It's new.
