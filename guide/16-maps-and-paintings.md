# Chapter 16 — Maps & Paintings

A blank map is a canvas. Draw on it, write on it, frame it on your wall.

### Paint a Map

Craft a map, hold it, and type `/map create <size>` to turn it into a canvas.
The size is 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16. Bigger numbers mean bigger, blockier pixels.
`/map edit <size>` opens the drawing screen.
`/map write <line> <colour> <alignment> <text>` puts words on it; the alignment is L, C or R.
`/map copy` and `/map paste` move a design between maps.
`/map publish` locks the picture for good, so nobody can redraw it, including you.

- **Map Help:** `/map help`

### Where They Go

Item frames hold finished maps like paintings.
Town halls, shop signs, and trail markers all want one.
A published map in a frame is a landmark; put your name on it.
