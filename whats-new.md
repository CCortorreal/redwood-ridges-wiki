## Your first walk around camp

New arrivals get a self-guided tour of the camp: the board, the Ranger Station, storage, the
craft room, the graveyard, the cabins, the market, then back to the board. A gold waypoint
marker points you to each stop in turn, and a sidebar tracks how many you've hit. Marshmallows
land in your pocket along the way, and the tour closes by pointing you at your first real job on
the board. Nothing about it is required reading; just walk where the marker points.

## The market square (spawn)

- **The Auctioneer.** Right-click the Auctioneer at the market square (the villager, or the market
  block itself) to open the auction house: list an item, set a price, buyers pay Marshmallows. A 1 %
  fee is burned on each sale — the camp's cut, not a player's. Typing `/ah` anywhere else tells you
  to go see the Auctioneer. No magic pockets.
- **Stalls.** Six shop plots at the market square, 40 Marshmallows to lease. Put a chest on your
  plot and make it a shop (hit the chest with the item you're selling, type the price). Shops only
  work on shop plots — no basement shops.
- Shops show up on the web map.

## Death's statue (the graveyard)

When you die, your grave keeps your stuff. **Praying at Death's statue** in the graveyard is the way
to be carried back to it — the `/graves` teleport is off.

- Right-click the statue (or Death, the figure beside it) → a card lists your graves → **Pray** on
  the one you want.
- **The toll:** 25 Marshmallows for your first death. It doubles each death in a streak
  (25 → 50 → 100 → 200 → 400 → 800 cap). Go 24 hours without dying and the streak resets.
- No Marshmallows, no ride — you can still walk to your grave the old-fashioned way.

## Ranks, the civic ladder

Powerless titles that show what you've done. Auto-granted; nobody has to ask.

| Rank | How you get it | Contracts at once |
|---|---|---|
| Seedling | You joined | 1 |
| Scout | Your first paid contract / cabin | 2 |
| Ranger | **Found a town** (instant), or 400 tracked payouts after 14 days here | 3 |
| Pathfinder | 1,200 tracked payouts and you're rooted — mayor of a town with 2+ residents, or a resident for 30 days, or 40 contracts solo | 5 |
| Scout Master | 3,000 tracked payouts, clean record, staff sign-off | 8 |

"Tracked payouts" means Marshmallows paid through the board (`/contract pay`). Tips from staff are
still tips — they don't move the bar. Founding a town is the fast road to Ranger.

**Warden is not a rank anymore** — it's the staff moderator role. You apply for it; Ranger is the
prerequisite.

## The board

- **`/contracts` from anywhere.** Rows are buttons. Click one to read the card, **Mark ready** when
  the work is done, or **Give up** your slot (no penalty, the slot reopens). Staff verify and pay.
- **Claiming still happens at the board** — click the book on the shelf at spawn.
- **Claim caps by rank** (table above). If a claim is refused, that's the cap — finish one or give
  one up.
- **`/contracts archive` and `/contracts ledger` are the same place now.** Click any book for the
  full job card — the original posting plus who got paid and when.
- Staff post contracts from an in-game form now (`/makecontract`) — expect more, smaller jobs.
- **Bedrock players:** contracts, payouts, ranks and crew cards all work with your dot-name.

## Runes & enchanting (SuperEnchants)

- **`/enchants`** (or `/runes`) is the guide: every custom enchant, where it comes from, what it does,
  what it conflicts with. Controller-friendly menu.
- **Hover to read.** Runed items carry their description in the lore, not just a name and level.
- **Rank helps at the table.** One rune always survives a roll. Your chance of a second one:
  Seedling 25 % · Scout 35 % · Ranger 45 % · Pathfinder 55 % · Scout Master 60 %, plus 10 % when
  another player is standing nearby (cap 70 %). Enchant together.
- **Excavator wears your pick.** The extra blocks it breaks now cost durability like they should.
- **A second family of runes, Ruin Runes, lives out in the world** — dungeon librarians and their
  loot chests, not the enchanting table. See the [field guide's Ruin Runes
  table](guide/14-runes-and-enchanting.md#ruin-runes) for the full list and where to find each one.

## Small fixes worth knowing about

- **Trim materials stack properly now** — fish, dye, and the rest of the trim set merge into one
  stack instead of splitting every time you pick more up.
- **Autoreel rods give fishing XP again**, same as reeling in by hand.
- **Advancements are fairer now.** "Kilometre Walk" no longer pops the moment you log in, and a

  no longer counts. Nothing you've already earned is taken away.

## The Adventurer's Guild (EliteMobs)

- `/ag` — the guild hub. Elite Coins are a separate currency: they don't touch Marshmallows.
- The **welcome tour pays 100 coins** (was 750). Ranks in the guild are earned in the guild.
- **Quest 2 gives an iron kit** (armor, sword, bow), not diamond, and **elite gear wears** in use.
- **Dungeons:** the Primis Adventure (levels 0-20), the Oasis Adventure (20-55), ten Story Mode
  dungeons and sanctums, plus the showpieces — Catacombs, Pirate Ship, Colosseum, Airship, Dark
  Cathedral, Vampire Manor, Steamworks, Beasts' Sanctuary, Goblin Kingdom, Yggdrasil, the Dark
  Spire. Difficulty scales to your level. Enchanting elite gear can drop you into a mini-challenge.
- You may be asked to accept a resource pack on join — say yes, it's the dungeon models.
